A 2-bedroom apartment selling at KES 9M in Syokimau, measuring 82.90 m² and finished to the same standard as the wider development. Semi-furnished, ready to move into, and on a sectional title. This is the most affordable entry point into the community. Every shared facility the development offers — the 6,000+ m² sky garden, the landscaped swimming pool, the fitness centre, the children's playground, the fingerprint-lock security standard, and the ground-floor retail podium — comes with this unit at the same level as the larger ones. What you give up is floor area and one bedroom, not the quality of the setting. The living and dining area is generously proportioned and opens onto a balcony — a feature the 3-bedroom Type D at KES 10M does not have. For a professional couple, a young family, or a downsizer, that outdoor space matters more than the extra bedroom, and this is the unit that gets it at the lowest price in the development. The kitchen is fitted with an inbuilt cooker and oven, with a dedicated washing area alongside. A water heater serves the unit. Security across the development runs at fingerprint-lock standard, with on-site property management and round-the-clock coverage. The commercial podium on the ground level includes restaurants, supermarkets, bars, and personalised shops — daily errands are a walk downstairs rather than a drive. Syokimau sits directly on the Mombasa Road corridor, minutes from JKIA and within easy reach of Gateway Mall, major banks, hospitals, and international schools. The commute into Nairobi's industrial area or the airport corridor is short and predictable. For landlords, this location has consistently strong rental demand from airline staff, corporate tenants, and professionals working in the airport corridor. Two parking spaces are allocated to the unit. Semi-furnished. Ready to move in. Sectional title. Viewing by appointment.
