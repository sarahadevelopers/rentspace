A 3-bedroom, 2-bathroom apartment selling at KES 11.8M in Syokimau, measuring 100 m², with two balconies and an open-plan living and dining area. Semi-furnished, ready to move into, and on a sectional title. What sets this unit apart from the rest of the development is the outdoor space. Two separate balconies on a 100 m² apartment is unusual — most units at this size get one, or none. It means the main living area can open onto one balcony while the master bedroom opens onto its own, giving genuine separation between indoor living and private retreat. For buyers who entertain, or who simply want cross-ventilation and natural light on two sides, this is the layout that delivers it. The open-style living and dining area is generously proportioned for the footprint and flows directly onto the main balcony. The master bedroom is served by its own ensuite bathroom, and the two remaining bedrooms share the second bathroom. The kitchen is fitted with an inbuilt cooker and oven, with a dedicated washing area alongside. A water heater serves the unit. Security across the development runs at fingerprint-lock standard, with on-site property management and round-the-clock coverage. Residents share a 6,000+ m² sky garden, a landscaped swimming pool, a fitness centre, and a children's playground. The commercial podium on the ground level includes restaurants, supermarkets, bars, and personalised shops — everyday needs are a walk downstairs rather than a drive. Syokimau sits directly on the Mombasa Road corridor, minutes from JKIA and within easy reach of Gateway Mall, major banks, hospitals, and international schools. The commute into Nairobi's industrial area or the airport corridor is short and predictable. For landlords, this location has consistently strong rental demand from airline staff, corporate tenants, and professionals working along the airport corridor. Two parking spaces are allocated to the unit. Semi-furnished. Ready to move in. Sectional title. Viewing by appointment.
