A single-storey 3-bedroom bungalow in Yukos, Kitengela — fully ensuite, set within a gated community, and finished to a standard that justifies the asking rent. For families with young children, or anyone who simply prefers not to live with stairs, this is the layout that rarely comes up in Kitengela at this level of finish. Every bedroom has its own bathroom. That means no morning queues, no shared facilities, and genuine privacy for each member of the household. The master suite sits apart from the other two bedrooms, and all three are generously sized with natural light on two sides. The living space is open-plan but properly zoned — a gracious lounge that flows into the dining area, with doors opening onto a private garden. The kitchen is well designed with porcelain tile worktops and a separate utility area, and there is a visitors' cloakroom off the entrance hall for guests. Interiors combine natural and painted walls, giving each room its own character rather than the flat, uniform finish you see in most rentals at this price. The community is fully serviced and secured. Borehole water supply with backup storage tanks — no rationing, no reliance on the mains. Solar water heating and solar panels keep running costs down. WiFi connectivity is available across the development, street lighting runs throughout, and the compound is cabro-paved, well-manicured, and secured by an electric perimeter wall with controlled gated access. There is a large common entertainment area and a dedicated children's play area, plus designated garbage disposal points. Yukos sits on the Nairobi–Namanga corridor with quick access to Kitengela town for shopping, banking, and dining. Acacia Crest School and Kitengela International School are both within easy reach, which makes the location practical as well as secure. Suited to a family, a professional couple, or anyone relocating to Kitengela who wants a completed home rather than a project. Available at KES 60,000 per month.
