Why Live in Syokimau?

Known for its peaceful environment and excellent amenities, Syokimau is a top choice for families and professionals. The area boasts reputable schools, modern hospitals, and convenient transport links.

Why rent this home?

Syokimau has become one of Nairobi's most sought-after residential areas — and for good reason. With excellent road and rail connectivity, proximity to JKIA and the SGR terminus, and a growing supply of modern developments, it offers the perfect balance of urban convenience and suburban calm. For buyers, it means a home that appreciates while you live in it. For investors, it means strong rental demand from professionals working in the CBD, at the airport, or along the Mombasa Road corridor. This particular 2-bedroom apartment stands out for one simple reason: space. At 112 sqm, it offers significantly more living area than the typical 2-bedroom unit in Syokimau, which usually sits around 80-95 sqm. That extra 20-30 sqm translates into genuinely comfortable living — a proper dining area, a spacious lounge, and bedrooms that don't feel cramped. The development adds a full lifestyle package on top: swimming pool, gym, children's play area, rooftop terrace, landscaped gardens, 24/7 security, backup generator, and reliable water supply. All the amenities of the 3-bedroom units in the same building, at a lower entry price. That combination — space, lifestyle, and location at KES 9.1M — makes this one of the strongest-value apartments currently available in the Kitengela-Syokimau corridor.
